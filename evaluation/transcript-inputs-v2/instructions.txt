TRANSCRIPT INPUTS V2 - LABELED FOR REVIEW

20 synthetic conversations: 5 Hindi, 5 Kannada, 5 English and 5 Malayalam.
All prices are in INR. Reference call time for every synthetic case: 25 November 2026, 10:00 AM Asia/Kolkata. Use manifest.json for these metadata; transcript files contain only ordered speaker turns.

You: person answering the service phone. Agent: booking assistant.
These are invented examples, not recordings, verified translations or new real customer calls. A fluent speaker should review Hindi, Kannada and Malayalam wording before treating labels as a benchmark.

Scenarios include combined charges, conditional inspection charges, changed quotes, labour-versus-parts costs, uncertain dates, withdrawn availability, wrong-person answers, service refusals and four disconnected calls. Some conversations resolve clearly after corrections; others remain genuinely unanswerable. Do not force exact values from every example.

Assistant-authored expected answers are stored separately in expected-labels.json. Labels await human approval. No model-generated test results or scores are included. Agent suggestions are not automatically provider confirmations. A missing answer or cut-off word is not an agreement. Preserve unresolved ambiguity in later labels.

The 10 real database transcripts remain separately in output/evaluation/real-transcripts/ (Git ignored). They retain their own real call timestamps, not this synthetic reference date. Existing older evaluation data and results were not changed.

Keep future answer labels separate from the model input. For date interpretation use the supplied reference time, without inventing a date when the conversation contradicts itself. Transcript-only evaluation cannot establish what was really spoken in the real call audio.

enough_info policy version 3: yes means a supported visit offer with a clear day, useful arrival period (including morning, evening or a window), and a clear applicable visit fee or total-job quote. Exact clock time and a separate visit fee are not required. Free inspection qualifies when explicit. Unresolved fee eligibility, tentative or contradictory scheduling, and agent-only guesses do not qualify. A clear total-job estimate can qualify if its scope and estimated nature remain explicit. See evaluation/enough-info-policy.json. This is information for user review, not booking confirmation.

The field currently exists only in evaluation labels. The production extraction schema, database and UI have not been changed. A future test must either derive it from extracted facts using this policy or explicitly request it in an evaluation-only model schema. Never pass the answer key or rationale to the tested model.

Exact-match targets: provider_response, visit_fee, repair_fee_min, repair_fee_max, visit_date, visit_time, enough_info. Assess free-text fields semantically, preserving caveats rather than enforcing exact wording. Materially ambiguous real-call labels are marked needs_adjudication and should be resolved before strict scoring.

Policy v3 labels include enough_info_basis outside expected, explaining timing and cost. visit_time stays null for broad periods. Combined costs never overwrite visit_fee or repair-only bounds.
