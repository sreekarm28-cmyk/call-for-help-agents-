Agent: For the noisy pump, customer can do Thursday at six. Budget is nine hundred.
You: Six won't work. Friday, seven fifteen should be fine. Visit is four hundred.
Agent: Friday at seven fifteen in the evening for four hundred?
You: Morning. And four hundred is my usual rate. That block is another fifty to come out.
Agent: Friday at seven fifteen a.m., four fifty for the visit?
You: Yes, put that down. If the relay is gone, that's another nine hundred or so.
