enough_info policy version 3: yes means a supported visit offer with a clear day, useful arrival period (including morning, evening or a window), and a clear applicable visit fee or total-job quote. Unresolved fee eligibility, tentative or contradictory scheduling, and agent-only guesses do not qualify. A clear total-job estimate can qualify if its scope and estimated nature remain explicit. See evaluation/enough-info-policy.json. This is information for user review, not booking confirmation.
